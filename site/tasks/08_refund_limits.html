Limit refunds to the captured amount

Allow partial refunds up to the captured amount, which may be less than what was authorized.

Python · SQLitemedium

What the hardened grader checks

  • Partial refunds add up and cannot exceed the capture
  • The ceiling is the captured amount, not the authorized amount
  • A rejected refund does not use up the remaining budget
  • Uncaptured and unknown payments are refused
  • Random refund sequences match a simple remaining-budget model

The ticket

Ticket: refund only captured funds

refunds.service.RefundService(db_path) uses SQLite and exposes create_payment(authorized_minor)->id, capture(id,amount_minor), refund(id,amount_minor)->refund_id, refunded_total(id)->int. Exceptions: RefundExceedsCaptured, PaymentNotCaptured, InvalidAmount (all derive from ValueError).

All money is integer minor units in one currency (CAD, 2 dp), never bool. Authorization, capture and refund amounts must be positive ints, otherwise InvalidAmount. An unknown payment id raises ValueError from capture, refund and refunded_total (after the amount has been validated). Each payment may be captured once, at most its authorization; invalid/repeated capture -> InvalidAmount. Refund before capture -> PaymentNotCaptured. Partial refunds are allowed. The cumulative successful refund amount may not exceed the captured amount, which may be less than authorized. Exceeding remaining captured funds -> RefundExceedsCaptured. Rejected attempts must never count toward refunded_total or reduce the future budget. Validate positive amount before capture status.

Refund ids are distinct positive ints. Capture and successful refunds survive a new service instance on the same file. Operations are atomic; no rejected operation may alter successful totals. Worked example: create_payment(10000), capture(id, 6000). refund(id, 4000) succeeds and refunded_total(id) == 4000. refund(id, 2500) then raises RefundExceedsCaptured, because only 2000 of the captured 6000 remains, and refunded_total(id) is still 4000.

No settlement network or real customer is involved. Preserve the interface and run python -m pytest tests -q from starter.
